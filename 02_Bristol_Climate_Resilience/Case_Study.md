## The place

Bristol Borough is a 1.6 square mile waterfront borough on the Delaware River in southern Bucks County, Pennsylvania, 23 miles northeast of Philadelphia. It carries a strong industrial history, a tight-knit community, and a historic grid that predates modern stormwater infrastructure. Its last comprehensive plan dates to 2006.

## What the plan does

Bristol 2050 is a long-range plan built on four pillars. In 2050, Bristol is Resilient, Accessible, Vibrant, and Equitable. The book opens with an existing-conditions diagnostic covering demographics, socioeconomics, zoning, land use, housing, transportation, and environment, then moves through issues and opportunities into a vision statement, five goals, and ten strategies with twelve sub-strategies. Two major projects combine multiple strategies.

The strategies range from mixed-use rezoning, a height and density bonus, a land bank, and accessory dwelling units to traffic safety management, parking reform, streetscape improvements, slowing down runoff, and park quality upgrades.

## My contribution

I led the environmental analysis and produced cartography for the plan, including watershed, flood, and tree canopy mapping that grounds the runoff and greening strategies.

## Why it matters for a portfolio

The book shows end-to-end planning capacity. It reads a place from data, translates that diagnostic into strategy, and presents the argument as a publication-quality document suitable for municipal client review.
