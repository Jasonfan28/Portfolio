PhillyStat360: Vacant Property Indicator
Practicum · Machine Learning
A parcel-level model scoring Philadelphia's 520,000 residential parcels for vacancy risk, built to find vacant homes that enforcement records miss. Calibrated logistic regression and random forest blended into a 50/50 ensemble, validated with spatially grouped cross-validation, and audited for flag rates across poverty quintiles before deployment.
