# 35 Years of Arctic Sea Ice Melt

**Map poster | Cartography | December 2024**

**Role:** Sole author
**Audience fit:** Cartography, environmental communication

## The map

A single-sheet polar-projection poster showing the retreat of Arctic sea ice between 1980 and 2015. July sea-ice observations are drawn at five-year intervals, with hatched fills stepping from 1980 through 2010 and the 2015 extent shown in solid white. The shrinking footprint of each successive layer makes the 35-year loss legible at a glance.

## Design choices

The basemap sits on a dark starfield with a muted polar land layer, so the ice extents carry the visual weight. A scale bar, graticule, and July-observation legend keep the piece readable as a reference document rather than only an illustration.

## Sources

Map layers credit Esri, USGS, NASA, NGA, and OpenStreetMap contributors, as noted on the sheet. Final layout was produced in Adobe Illustrator.
