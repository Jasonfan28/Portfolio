2026 ◆ City of Philadelphia

PhillyStat360: Vacant Property Indicator

A parcel-level model scoring Philadelphia's 520,000 residential parcels for vacancy risk, built to find the vacant homes enforcement records never catch. Calibrated logistic regression and random forest blended into a 50/50 ensemble, validated with spatially grouped cross-validation, and audited for flag rates across poverty quintiles before deployment.
