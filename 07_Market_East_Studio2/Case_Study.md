# Reimagining Market East

**Studio 2 vision plan | December 2025**

**Role:** Contributor, existing-conditions data analysis and cartography
**Team:** 12-person studio team
**Course:** CPLN 7020 Studio 2 (University of Pennsylvania)
**Site:** Census Tract 5, Center City Philadelphia (Market East corridor)
**Audience fit:** Planning and design, housing and real estate, public policy

## The site

Market East is Philadelphia's historic retail spine between City Hall and Old City, anchored by the Fashion District, the Convention Center, and Reading Terminal Market. A century of cycles, from department-store prosperity through the decline of the Gallery to the Fashion District reopening in 2019, has left a corridor that people pass through more than they linger in. The studio brief asked for a 2050 vision.

## The process

The book moves through existing conditions, alternatives, guiding values, and strategies. Existing conditions cover the street's past and present, who lives and works in the corridor, and what daily life there feels like. Four guiding values carry through the plan: Human-Centered, Diverse, Central, and Connected.

## The strategies

Nine strategies sit under the four values. Human-Centered covers welcoming spaces and inclusive design. Diverse covers housing and commercial offerings. Central covers commercial impact, event programming, and the creation of third spaces. Connected covers safer street design and enhancing corridor connection. Each strategy ties to precedents and Philadelphia-specific implementation tools.

## My contribution

I contributed to the existing-conditions data work, including foot-traffic analysis using Advan monthly patterns via Dewey Data and the Census Tract 5 demographic baseline. That work supported the "Who's in Market East?" and "What Life is Like" spreads and fed the corridor-connection analysis.

## The 2050 vision

The plan frames Market East in 2050 as a resilient, vibrant downtown corridor that connects communities, commerce, and culture, drawing people to live, work, visit, and play.
