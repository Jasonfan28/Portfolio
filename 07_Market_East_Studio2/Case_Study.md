## The site

Market East is Philadelphia's historic retail spine between City Hall and Old City, anchored by the Fashion District, the Convention Center, and Reading Terminal Market. A century of cycles, from department-store era prosperity, through the decline of the Gallery, to the Fashion District reopening in 2019, has left a corridor that people pass through more than they linger in. The studio brief asked for a 2050 vision.

## The process

The book is organized around four phases: existing conditions, alternatives, guiding values, and strategies. Existing conditions cover the history of the street (1682 to present), current demographics and commercial mix, and what life actually feels like in the corridor today. Four guiding values (human-centered, diverse, central, connected) carry through each strategy.

## The strategies

Ten recommendations grouped under four headings:

- **Welcoming** — welcoming spaces, inclusive design
- **Diverse** — housing typology, commercial offerings
- **Central** — commercial impact, event programming, third-space creation
- **Connected** — safer street design, corridor connection

Each recommendation ties to a precedent drawn from global library and civic-space design, a Philadelphia-specific policy instrument (neighborhood improvement district amendment, development impact tax, Vision Zero capital plan), and a phased implementation path.

## My contribution

I contributed to existing-conditions data work, including Dewey visitation and POI analysis, OSM building-footprint processing, and the Census Tract 5 demographic baseline. That data work supported the "Who's in Market East?" and "What Life is Like" spreads in the existing-conditions chapter and underpinned the corridor-connection analysis in the strategies chapter.

## The 2050 vision

The closing spread frames Market East as a resilient and vibrant downtown corridor that connects communities, commerce, and culture. It draws people to live, work, visit, and play, and has established itself as an inclusive destination for all.
